Stargazing Basics

Start by learning the major constellations like Orion, Ursa Major, and Cassiopeia. Use a star map or an app to help you locate planets and bright stars. Begin in a dark area away from city lights.

Choosing a Telescope

For beginners, a simple refractor or Dobsonian telescope is perfect. Look for something with a stable mount and a clear eyepiece. Start with low magnification to make objects easier to find.

Astrophotography Tips

Use a tripod and a camera with manual settings. Start with long exposures and wide lenses. Try photographing the Moon first, then move on to star trails and deep-sky objects.
